About

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS), working with Prof. Ling Chen. I received my Ph.D. from The University of Queensland, supervised by Prof. Xin Yu.

My research focuses on agentic AI and multimodal learning. I am interested in how AI systems connect perception with reliable action: understanding sounds and visual scenes, grounding instructions in interfaces, and carrying out useful tasks in real-world environments.

My current work explores computer-use agents for long-horizon workflows and the diagnostic evaluation of multimodal foundation models. Previously, I developed object-centric audio-visual alignment methods for sounding-source segmentation. I have also worked with CSIRO Data61, NetEase Fuxi AI Lab and Alibaba Tongyi Lab.
